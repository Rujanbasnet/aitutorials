One week after the US government forced the most dramatic AI shutdown in history, Anthropic is signaling the end may be near.

At a press conference in Seoul on June 17, Chris Ciauri, Anthropic's Managing Director of International, told reporters: "We are very confident that in the coming days, the models will become available again." It is the most optimistic public statement from any Anthropic executive since [Fable 5 and Mythos 5 went dark on June 12](/news/2026-06-14-anthropic-fable-5-mythos-5-government-shutdown/).

## How the Ban Actually Happened

Reporting from [WIRED](https://www.wired.com) and The Washington Post has now revealed that the shutdown was a two-step process, not a single decision.

First, the White House identified SK Telecom — South Korea's largest carrier and a $100 million Anthropic investor — as a Chinese security risk with access to Mythos 5. That alone would have been a targeted revocation.

But then Amazon researchers independently flagged jailbreak vulnerabilities in Fable 5, and the situation escalated. David Sacks, co-chair of the President's AI council, reportedly gave Dario Amodei an ultimatum: fix the jailbreak or de-deploy the model. Amodei refused both, and the Commerce Department issued a blanket export control order covering all foreign nationals.

## Where Negotiations Stand

Senior Anthropic technical staff have been meeting with Commerce Department officials in Washington. National Cyber Director Sean Cairncross joined the working-level talks. Both Amodei and Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick are attending the G7 summit in Evian-les-Bains, France, which could provide another venue for resolution.

Meanwhile, Anthropic pressed ahead with opening its [Seoul office](https://www.anthropic.com/news/seoul-office-partnerships-korean-ai-ecosystem) — its third in Asia-Pacific — and announced partnerships with Samsung SDS, NAVER, and Nexon, even though Korean users currently cannot access the company's most powerful models.

## What This Means for You

If you subscribed during the [Fable 5 launch window](/news/2026-06-12-anthropic-claude-fable-5-launch/) (June 9–14), refunds are available — but the deadline is tomorrow, June 20.

The prediction market Polymarket currently gives a 57% chance that Fable 5 will be restored before July 1. We will update this story when access is officially restored.
