For the first time in the AI industry, Anthropic is not just keeping pace with OpenAI — it is pulling ahead.

The [June 2026 Ramp AI Index](https://ramp.com/leading-indicators/ai-index-june-2026) shows Anthropic now leads US business adoption at 41% of companies with paid AI subscriptions, up 2.5 percentage points from the previous month. OpenAI slipped slightly to 39.5%, down 0.1 points.

This is not a survey or a poll. Ramp tracks actual credit card and billing data across more than 50,000 US businesses, making it one of the most concrete measures of where companies are putting their AI budgets.

## How Did Anthropic Get Here?

The crossover first appeared in the May 2026 report, when Anthropic edged past OpenAI at 34.4% versus 32.3%. But the June data shows this was not a fluke — Anthropic has continued to grow while OpenAI has stalled.

The numbers are striking in context. Anthropic sat at just 9% of businesses in May 2025. In one year, it has more than quadrupled its reach. OpenAI, by contrast, grew its business adoption by only 0.3% over the same period.

Much of Anthropic's growth has been driven by the strength of its [Claude model family](/news/2026-06-12-anthropic-claude-fable-5-launch/), particularly for coding, research, and autonomous workflows. The recent launch of Claude Fable 5 — its most capable model yet — has only accelerated enterprise interest.
