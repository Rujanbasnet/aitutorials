The most dramatic AI shutdown in history is officially over. Anthropic [restored global access](https://www.anthropic.com/news/redeploying-fable-5) to Claude Fable 5 on July 1, 2026 — 19 days after the US Commerce Department ordered the model taken offline over a reported jailbreak vulnerability.

Fable 5 and its larger sibling Mythos 5 are now available again across Claude.ai, Claude Code, Cowork, and the API. The restoration follows the Commerce Department's decision on June 30 to lift the export controls it had imposed on June 12, just three days after the models launched.

## What Changed

The key difference is a new safety classifier. During the shutdown, Anthropic trained a smaller AI system that monitors conversations and detects when Fable 5 is asked to perform potentially harmful cybersecurity tasks. The classifier blocks the specific jailbreak — originally discovered by Amazon researchers — in over 99% of cases.

When the classifier triggers, users see a notification and their request is automatically rerouted to [Opus 4.8](/news/2026-04-16-anthropic-claude-opus-4-7-release/) instead. There is a trade-off: Anthropic acknowledges that the tighter filtering may also block some legitimate coding and security requests.

## The Deal With the Government

In exchange for lifting the ban, Anthropic agreed to three conditions: proactively detect and address security risks in its models, work with the government on testing protocols and release standards for current and future models, and report any observed malicious activity.

This framework could set the template for how future AI model disputes between companies and regulators are resolved — a negotiated set of safeguards rather than an outright ban.

## What This Means for You

If you were using Fable 5 before the shutdown, you can pick up where you left off. The model retains its position at the top of coding benchmarks, scoring 80% on SWE-Bench Pro. For most users, the experience will be identical — the new classifier runs in the background and only activates on specific cybersecurity-related prompts.

If you are new to Fable 5, now is a good time to try it. It remains [one of the most capable models available](/tutorials/prompt-engineering/) for complex reasoning, coding, and creative tasks.

The 19-day saga is a reminder that even the most powerful AI tools exist within a regulatory framework — and that framework is still being written.
