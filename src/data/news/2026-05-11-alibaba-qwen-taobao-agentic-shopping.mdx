Alibaba announced today that it is integrating its Qwen AI assistant directly into Taobao and Tmall, the company's two largest e-commerce marketplaces. The move replaces traditional keyword search with conversational shopping — users can now describe what they want in plain language and let the AI handle the rest.

## How It Works

From inside the Qwen app, shoppers can ask the AI agent to find a product, compare it across sellers, run a virtual try-on, monitor prices over 30 days, and place an order. The entire transaction completes through Alipay, with the AI agent stepping back only for final user confirmation.

The assistant has access to more than [four billion products across Taobao and Tmall](https://technode.com/2026/05/11/alibaba-to-merge-qwen-ai-with-taobao-in-push-for-new-ai-powered-conversational-shopping-experience/), and Alibaba has built a dedicated skills library that handles logistics tracking, customer service, and after-sales support. The system also draws on users' past orders and preferences to generate personalised recommendations.

One standout feature is automatic price-based purchasing: users can set a target price for an item, and the assistant will place the order automatically once that threshold is met.

## Why This Is a Big Deal

This is arguably the most ambitious deployment of [AI agents](/blog/what-are-ai-agents/) in consumer commerce so far. While Western platforms have added AI shopping features — ChatGPT's Shopify integration, Amazon's Rufus assistant — those tools mostly produce search-style answers. The actual buying still happens through separate checkout flows.

Alibaba's approach is different. The AI agent manages the full journey from product discovery to payment and delivery tracking, making it a true end-to-end agentic experience. With Qwen already at 300 million monthly active users across Alibaba's apps, this integration reaches an enormous audience from day one.

## What This Means for You

Even if you don't use Taobao, this signals where online shopping is heading globally. Instead of scrolling through pages of results and reading reviews, you'll increasingly tell an AI what you need and let it do the legwork.
